A fever dream, but really not in a bad sense. I loved this book, it's characters and it's atmosphere and it's attention to detail, even though I didnt manage to follow the plot or it's logic half the time. Nevertheless you just know Gibson has put a lot of thought into this, and as his friend Jack Womack wrote, captured precisely, what the future was going to miss.
