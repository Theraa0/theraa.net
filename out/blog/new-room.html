Ignore how I said I was going to write about moving here lol, i kinda forgot...

chaos

I think I moved over most of my stuff two weeks ago but that doesn't mean theres any less chaos here now. The girl who lived here before thankfully left me a bunch of stuff, but most of it is very impractical so that I barely have any space to put stuff apart from the floor. My dad helped me put in a desk at least, but that barely helped...

the kitchen

So far I don't even have a chair, barely even the space for one. I've been thinking though, it might be cool to just have a big armchair there, but I'll see what I can find for cheap. Recently I've been seeing a bunch of very green rooms on pinterest so I'm planning to get a bunch of plants, that entire hobby seems crazy tho, so I have a lot of reading up to do before anything in that regard can happen. The flower seeds I got from a friend are sprouting now tho!! The walls here also seem like a very nice chance to get some new posters and stickers, or maybe some grafitti, altough I heard it's supposed to be very smelly.

One thing noone told me about tho was how fucking expensive living on your own is. The ammount of random shit you suddenly need is insane n I can barely afford anything rn, so doing any decorating is put on hold until thats all figured out.

I really don't want to complain though, living near the city centre is literally changing my entire life here, cuz I suddenly don't need a one hour heads up on everything anymore and being able to do stuff without having to think about moving again soon is great.
